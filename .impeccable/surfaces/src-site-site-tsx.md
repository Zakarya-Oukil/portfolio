## Direction contract
THESIS: One engineering drawing sheet in dark paper: the viewport-fixed frame doubles as a scroll ruler, and every case study is a sheet stacked onto the last. Refuses generic project-card grids and stock hero layouts.
OWN-WORLD: Warm charcoal paper with static grain, bone ink, one red used for the tape, the eJPT stamp, brackets, scan line and marker; Gambetta serif for names and headings, Switzer for reading, JetBrains Mono only for measurement-style callouts, competency lines and sheet labels; square corners; double-line frame with rulers.
STORY: The visitor sees a person and a credible credential trail within seconds, learns the roles, then flips through real work sheets and reaches a channel of their choice; nothing unearned is shown as earned.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the serif name, one line, and the actions (Try the live demo in brick red, Email, Book 30 minutes); right, the rotated taped print with red corner brackets and leader lines to eJPT (red, certified), BTL1, Security+ and the Master's (plain, in progress); the frame draws itself around the viewport.
FORM: Mix of versions 1 to 5 as chosen by the owner; no concept roll was used. Signature interactions: halftone-develop print with cursor tilt, pinned stacked sheet reel with scan-line reveal and loupe on the one real screenshot, Flip role rows, ruler marker naming the section, sheet-wipe transitions, ScrollSmoother on wide fine-pointer screens only.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
